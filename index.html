SmartLens — Support

SmartLens is a smart camera combining photo, video, dual-camera recording, document scanning, text recognition, and powerful photo/video editors in one app.

FAQ

  • DUAL mode is missing: Dual-camera recording requires a device that supports simultaneous multi-camera capture (iPhone XS or later).
  • Where are my captures saved? Everything is saved to your device's Photos library.
  • Is my data safe? Yes — everything runs on-device and no data is collected. See the Privacy Policy.
